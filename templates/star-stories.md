# STAR Stories — Interview-Ready Examples

Organized by competency. Each story maps to one or more of your role archetypes. Build this library over time — aim for 2-3 stories per competency, with different stories highlighting different archetypes.

**STAR format:**
- **Situation:** The context — what was the starting state?
- **Task:** What were you asked or expected to do?
- **Action:** What did *you specifically* do? (Not your team — you.)
- **Result:** What happened? Quantify wherever possible.

## Tips for Building Your Story Library

1. **Start with your proof points.** Your positioning briefs already have key accomplishments — expand each into a full STAR story.
2. **Practice out loud.** A story that reads well may not flow when spoken. Aim for 60-90 seconds per story.
3. **Map stories to common interview questions.** "Tell me about a time you..." questions always map to a competency above.
4. **Vary the recency.** Have stories from different career stages — don't rely only on your most recent role.
5. **Know the short version.** For each story, also prepare a 2-sentence summary version for when the question calls for a quick example, not a deep dive.
6. **Tag the archetypes.** Each story should note which of your archetypes it supports — some stories serve multiple archetypes with different emphasis.
