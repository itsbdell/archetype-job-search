# Outreach Templates

Starting points for common outreach scenarios. Always personalize — these are structural patterns, not copy-paste scripts.

---

## Template 1: Warm Reconnection

**Use when:** You know the person, haven't talked recently, and want to catch up in the context of your search.

Subject: Quick update + a question

Hey [Name],

Hope you're doing well. I'm [brief transition context — e.g., "exploring what's next after X"] and looking at roles where I can [brief positioning — what you want to do next].

I'd love to hear what you're seeing in [their domain/industry]. Would you have 20 minutes in the next couple weeks?

Best,
[Your name]

---

## Template 2: Cold Outreach (Role-Specific)

**Use when:** You don't know them but want to connect about a specific open role.

Subject: [Company] — [Role Title]

Hi [Name],

I came across the [Role Title] position at [Company] and wanted to reach out directly. I'm currently [your current/recent role], where I [1-sentence relevant accomplishment].

[1 sentence connecting their company/mission to your interest — be specific about *what* interests you.]

I'd love to learn more about the role and how [Company] is thinking about [relevant topic]. Would you be open to a brief conversation?

Best,
[Your name]
[your website or LinkedIn]

---

## Template 3: Referral Request

**Use when:** Asking someone in your network to introduce you.

Subject: Quick favor — job search

Hey [Name],

I'm actively searching and targeting [archetype description — e.g., "product strategy roles at growth-stage tech companies"]. I know you're connected to [company/person/domain] — would you be comfortable making an introduction? Happy to send you a blurb you can forward.

Appreciate it either way.

[Your name]

---

## Template 4: Follow-Up After Application

**Use when:** You've applied and want to connect with someone at the company.

Subject: Following up — [Role] at [Company]

Hi [Name],

I applied for the [Role Title] position at [Company] last week and wanted to follow up. I'm especially interested in [specific thing about the role/company that connects to your experience].

My background in [1-2 relevant proof points] maps directly to what you're building. I'd welcome the chance to discuss how I could contribute.

Best,
[Your name]

---

## Template 5: Thank You / Post-Conversation

**Use when:** After an informational chat, interview, or helpful connection.

Subject: Thanks for the time

[Name],

Really appreciated the conversation today. [1 specific thing you learned or found interesting — shows you were listening.]

[If applicable: I'll follow up on [thing you discussed] — [specific next action].]

Looking forward to staying in touch.

[Your name]

---

## Template 6: LinkedIn Connection Request

**Use when:** Sending a LinkedIn connection request to someone you don't know.

Hi [Name] — I'm a [brief positioning] exploring [archetype description] roles. I noticed [specific signal — their work, company, a post they wrote] and would love to connect. [One sentence of context on why.]

---

## Usage Notes

- **Always personalize.** A generic message is worse than no message.
- **Keep it short.** 3-4 sentences for the core message. Long cold emails don't get read.
- **Be specific about the ask.** "20 minutes to chat" is better than "I'd love to connect sometime."
- **Reference something real.** Their recent work, a mutual connection, a specific thing about the company. Not "I'm impressed by your innovative company."
- **Follow up once** after 5-7 days if no response, then once more after another week, then move on.
- **Match the channel.** LinkedIn messages are shorter than emails. Twitter DMs are shorter still.
