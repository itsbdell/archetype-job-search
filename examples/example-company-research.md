## What They Do

## Why They're Interesting
Moving from "infrastructure tool" to "infrastructure platform" — they're at the exact inflection point where product strategy and positioning matter most. The AI orchestration product line is new (launched Q4 2025) and needs someone who can define the category. NYC office is growing, and they're hiring senior product leaders for the first time outside SF. Public statements from their CEO about moving from "engineering-led" to "product-led" growth signal a strategic shift.
